Our Story

We know Tanzania because this is home.

Kwai PM Kwai Travel and Tours Limited was built around a simple idea: travellers deserve more than a basic tour package. They deserve a team that understands the seasons, the roads, the lodges, the guides, and the rhythm of each destination — and knows how to bring it all together with care.

We help guests explore Tanzania through luxury safaris, Zanzibar beach holidays, Kilimanjaro experiences, cultural tours, and custom-made itineraries. Whether you are travelling as a couple, a family, or a private group, we design the journey around how you want to move, rest, and discover.

The brand has a premium African safari feel — warm, elegant, trustworthy, and experience-driven. We are here to make Tanzania feel welcoming, seamless, and unforgettable.
